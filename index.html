Healthy study habits

  • 💧 Keep water nearby and drink regularly.
  • 🥗 Eat balanced meals with protein, vegetables and carbohydrates.
  • 🚶 Take short movement breaks between study sessions.
  • 😴 Aim for enough sleep, especially before exams.
  • 🍎 Choose a fruit or another nourishing snack when hungry.

These are general wellness suggestions, not personalized medical advice.
